# Private negotiation playbook — Liability

## Standard position
- Cap the supplier's aggregate liability at **12 months of fees** paid under the agreement in the 12 months before the claim.
- Cap should apply to contract, tort, and statutory claims to the extent permitted by law.
- Do **not** accept unlimited liability for ordinary negligence or service failures.

## Carve-outs (acceptable)
- Fraud, wilful misconduct, and death/personal injury caused by negligence may remain uncapped where required by law.
- Confidentiality breaches and IP infringement indemnities may sit outside the cap **only** if the commercial team approves.

## Redlines to push
- If the contract has no liability cap, insert a 12-month fees cap.
- If the cap is higher than 12 months (e.g. 24 months or annual contract value × 2), reduce it to 12 months of fees.
- If liability is unlimited except for excluded heads, add an aggregate cap of 12 months of fees for all other claims.

## Language preference
Prefer: "the Supplier's total aggregate liability arising under or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by the Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim."
