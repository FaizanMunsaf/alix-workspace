# Private negotiation playbook — Confidentiality

## Standard position
- Confidentiality obligations must be **mutual**.
- Survival: confidentiality should survive termination for at least **three (3) years**, or indefinitely for trade secrets.
- Residuals / general skills exclusions are acceptable if narrowly drafted.

## Redlines to push
- If only one party is bound, make the clause mutual.
- If survival is missing or shorter than 3 years (and not trade-secret perpetual), extend survival to 3 years.
- Reject broad customer-only disclosure rights without reciprocal protection for supplier confidential information.
