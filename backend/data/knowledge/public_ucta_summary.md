# Public legal reference — UK unfair contract terms (summary stub)

This is a **public/legal reference mock** for architecture demo (not legal advice).

## Consumer / B2C note
Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and related unfair terms regimes, clauses that exclude or limit liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence are generally not enforceable against consumers.

## B2B reasonableness (UCTA 1977 — high level)
In many B2B contracts governed by English law, exclusions and limitations of liability for negligence may need to satisfy a reasonableness test under the Unfair Contract Terms Act 1977 where they apply.

## Drafting implication for demos
- Prefer express carve-outs for death/personal injury caused by negligence.
- Caps expressed as a multiple of fees are common commercial practice; reasonableness still depends on bargaining power and circumstances.
